Barriers, Work, and Returns:
A Unified Socioeconomic Analytical Framework

壁垒、做功与收益：一个统一的社会经济分析框架
Lei Dai
作者：戴磊
理论构建论文 · 中英双语逐段对照版 · Bilingual paragraph-aligned web edition
Abstract: Starting from a fundamental question — how do human markets come into existence — this paper constructs a unified socioeconomic analytical framework. We treat trade as a state flow in which society moves from diversity toward sameness, define a barrier as an existing structure that maintains a potential differential against the homogenizing trend, and argue that a barrier holder's return is the compensation for its maintenance cost. The framework's main components are: (1) a three-dimensional description of barriers (breadth of domain, depth of subject-embeddedness, and the time structure of tolls), together with a seven-stage sequence of attack, defense, and retreat along the embeddedness axis; (2) a unified "work–crystallization" theory that classifies individual capability and structural power as two phases of the same flow of work in two kinds of containers, yielding a four-variable representation of returns; and (3) boundary-layer propositions: homogenization is asymptotic rather than terminal, the lower bound of differentiation follows from the non-homogenizability of decision rights plus physical constraints, and automation is an endogenous, ever-lengthening river of externalizing work. On this basis we derive six strategy rules and test the framework against sixteen sets of documentary evidence and historical cases (hand-loom weavers, bank tellers and ATMs, radiology, nurses versus star doctors, r>g, among others); propositions for which no direct evidence yet exists and which rest on inference are honestly flagged with an orange (†) throughout the text. The paper's contribution is to unify three economic traditions — division of labor, transaction costs, and rent — on a single axis (subject-embeddedness), and to provide falsifiable, structured predictions for individual and organizational decision-making in the AI era.
Keywords: transaction costs; barrier rent; work and crystallization; automation; labor markets in the AI era
摘要：本文从一个根本问题出发——人类交易市场是如何产生的——构建了一个统一的社会经济分析框架。我们将交易视为社会从多样性向同一性转变的状态流动，将壁垒定义为逆均一化趋势而维持势差的存在结构，并论证壁垒持有者的收益是对其维持成本的补偿。框架的主要构件包括：（1）壁垒的三维描述（作用域广度、主体嵌入度、收费时间结构）与沿嵌入度轴的七级攻防退守序列；（2）"做功—结晶"统一理论，将个人能力与结构性权力归为同一做功流在两类容器中的相态，给出收益的四变量函数表示；（3）边界层命题：均一化渐近而非到达，差异下界由决策权不可均分与物理约束给出，自动化是做功体外化的内生长河。基于框架，我们推导出六条选择策略，并用十六组文献证据与历史案例（手工织布工、ATM 柜员、放射科、护士与名医、r>g 等）进行检验；暂无直接证据、依赖推理的命题在文中以橙色(†)诚实标注。本文的贡献在于以单一坐标轴（主体嵌入度）统一了分工、交易费用与租金三条经济学传统，并为 AI 时代的个人与组织决策提供可证伪的结构化预测。
关键词：交易费用；壁垒租金；做功与结晶；自动化；AI 时代的劳动市场

1 Introduction　1 引言

1.1 Where the Question Starts　1.1 问题的起点

How do markets for human trade come into being? The standard answer appeals to division of labor and self-interest: individuals produce different goods and profit by exchanging them. This paper adopts a more fundamental perspective: trade can be regarded as a state flow in which human society moves from diversity toward sameness; the mode of trade is infrastructure, and transaction costs are the cost of flowing across a given infrastructure — the compensation for flattening differences. Historically, the barrier that acts as the main cost of flow keeps rotating: in the agrarian age it was the transport barrier; as technology advanced it became the information barrier; in the software era it became the production barrier. As generative AI pushes the cost of "generation" toward zero, the production barrier is collapsing — what will be the most expensive thing next?

人类之间的交易市场是如何产生的？标准回答诉诸分工与自利：个体生产不同商品，通过交换获利。本文采取更根本的视角：交易可视为人类社会从多样性向同一性转变的状态流动；交易方式是基础设施，交易费用是在特定基础设施上进行状态流动的成本，即抹平差异性的补偿。历史上，充当主要流动成本的壁垒不断轮动：农耕时代是运输壁垒，技术进步后转为信息壁垒，软件时代转为生产壁垒。随着生成式人工智能将"生成"的成本推向零，生产壁垒正在崩塌——下一个最贵的是什么？

Pursuing this question to the end yields three fundamental questions: (1) How does the tension between differentiation and sameness drive social change? (2) What determines an individual's returns in this process? (3) How should an individual choose? This paper answers them in turn with the framework (Section 3), a theory of choice (Section 4), and empirical tests (Section 5).

沿此追问到底，得到三个根本问题：（1）差异性与同一性的张力如何驱动社会变化？（2）个体在该过程中的收益由什么决定？（3）个体应如何做选择？本文依次给出框架（第3节）、选择理论（第4节）与证据检验（第5节）。

1.2 Relation to Economic Traditions　1.2 与经济学传统的关系

The framework unifies three traditions: Smith's theory of the division of labor (the spatial distribution of differences), Coase's theory of transaction costs (barriers define the boundary of the market), and Ricardo's theory of rent (scarce barriers yield rent). Schumpeter's innovation rent, Polanyi's tacit knowledge, Bourdieu's conversion of forms of capital, and North's institutional theory then serve, respectively, as key inputs to the framework's dynamic layer, structural layer, unifying layer, and institutional boundary.

框架统一了三条传统：斯密的分工理论（差异的空间分布）、科斯的交易费用理论（壁垒决定市场边界）、李嘉图的租金理论（稀缺壁垒产生租）。其后熊彼特的创新租金、波兰尼的默会知识、布迪厄的资本形态转换、诺思的制度理论，分别构成本框架的动力层、结构层、统一层与制度边界的重要输入。

1.3 Methodological Note　1.3 方法论说明

This paper is a systematic attempt at theory construction: start from observation and propose a unifying hypothesis; converge the framework through sustained dialogue with historical and contemporary cases; then derive strategies and submit them to stress-testing by counterexamples. Every empirical claim is given a documentary or data source wherever possible; propositions for which no direct evidence can be found are flagged with a (†) and their nature is clarified collectively in Section 6.

本文是一次系统化的理论构建：从观察出发提出统一假设，通过与历史及当代案例的持续对话收敛框架，再推导策略并接受反例的压力测试。全部经验断言均尽量给出文献或数据出处；无法找到直接证据的命题以(†)标注，并在第6节集中说明其性质。

2 Concepts and Definitions　2 概念定义

  • Potential differential (势差): the differential distribution of the world along dimensions such as goods, information, capability, and trust. Differentials drive flows (trade, learning, imitation) and are, in this paper's terms, the prime mover of social change.
  • Barrier (壁垒): an existing structure that maintains a potential differential against the homogenizing flow. The barrier holder pays a maintenance cost, and its return is the compensation for that cost (rent).
  • Work (做功): the sustained input of labor, attention, and energy — the "currency" of all barriers.
  • Crystallization (结晶): the conversion of flowing work, through a registration system (property rights, diplomas, brands, platform weights, etc.), into a stock that can be stored, transferred, and inherited.
  • Container (容器): the place where a crystallization is registered. A container can be an individual body, an organization, or an institutional position; container ownership determines the security and inheritability of the stock.
  • Depreciation (折旧): the decay of a stock through forgetting, institutional change, and network migration; maintenance-type work is used to hedge against depreciation.
  • 势差：世界在物、信息、能力、信任等维度上的差异分布。势差驱动流（交易、学习、模仿），是本文所称社会变化的原动力。
  • 壁垒：逆均一化流向而维持势差的存在结构。壁垒持有者付出维持成本，其收益即对该成本的补偿（租金）。
  • 做功：劳动、注意力与能量的持续投入，是一切壁垒的"货币"。
  • 结晶：流动的做功经登记系统（产权、文凭、品牌、平台权重等）转化为可储存、可转移、可继承的存量。
  • 容器：结晶的登记处所，可以是个人身体、组织或制度位置；容器所有权决定存量的安全与可继承性。
  • 折旧：存量因遗忘、制度变迁与网络迁移而衰减的过程；维护型做功用于对冲折旧。

3 The Framework　3 框架

3.1 Ontological Layer: Differentials and the Prime Mover　3.1 本体层：势差与原动力

Differentials exist in the world, and differentials drive flows. Homogenization is the prime mover of social change, but it does not produce monotonic convergence: barriers maintain differentials against the flow, so there is persistent pressure on both sides of every barrier, and society pays two kinds of cost at the barrier — the cost of crossing (transaction costs) and the cost of maintenance. The barrier holder's return is the compensation for the maintenance cost.

世界存在势差，势差驱动流。均一化是社会变化的原动力，但不导致单调收敛：壁垒逆着流维持势差，于是壁垒两侧存在持续压强，社会在壁垒处付出两种成本——跨越成本（交易费用）与维持成本。壁垒持有者的收益即维持成本的补偿。

3.2 Structural Layer: Three Dimensions and the Sequence of Attack and Retreat　3.2 结构层：壁垒三维与攻防序列

The complete description of a barrier is a product of three dimensions: breadth of domain × depth of subject-embeddedness × time structure of tolls. Attack, defense, and retreat form a continuous sequence along the embeddedness axis:

壁垒的完整描述为三个维度之积：作用域广度 × 主体嵌入度 × 收费时间结构。攻防退守沿嵌入度轴构成连续序列：

Goods → Information → Knowledge → Trust → Interpretation rights → Governance rights → Decision rights

物 → 信息 → 知识 → 信任 → 解释权 → 治理权 → 决策权

The distinction between adjacent levels reduces to a single axis: the depth to which the barrier is embedded in the subject (detachability decreasing), whose side facets include: decreasing replicability, decreasing verifiability, increasing consensus requirements, increasing replacement cost, and a decreasing capacity to "collect rent while lying down." Homogenization's tactic against relational barriers is to objectify first (trust → scores, law → code) and homogenize second; retreat therefore keeps advancing toward meta-levels.

相邻层级的区别归约于一条主轴：壁垒嵌入主体的深度（离体性递减），其侧面包括可复制性递减、可验证性递减、共识需求递增、替换成本递增、"躺着收租"能力递减。均一化对关系性壁垒的战术是先对象化（信任→评分、法律→代码）、后均一化，退守因此持续向元层推进。

3.3 Dynamic Layer: Cost, Competition, and Infrastructure　3.3 动力层：成本、竞争与基础设施

The rent-maximizing form of a barrier is a controlled leak: fully impermeable and the flow disappears; fully permeable and the differential disappears; the rent-maximizing calibration point lies in between. Infrastructure is a composite of "toll collection + wall-demolition services"; competition among barriers of the same kind is decided by net barrier cost — the winner's rent minus the value of its wall-demolition service to others must be smaller than the network's cost of building an alternative(†). When domains do not fully overlap, old barriers survive on residual dimensions (cash in anonymous scenarios of the e-payment era). The replacement channel of institutional barriers is political, with lags of up to a century — the framework has directional but not temporal predictive power at the institutional layer.

租金最大化的壁垒形态是受控渗漏：完全不可逾越则流消失，完全可逾越则势差消失，租金最大化的校准点在中途。基础设施是"收费＋拆壁服务"的复合体；同类壁垒之间的竞争由净壁垒成本决定——胜者收取的租金与其拆除其他壁垒的服务价值之差，须小于网络自建替代方案的成本(†)。作用域不完全重叠时，旧壁垒在残余维度续命（现金在电子支付时代的匿名场景）。制度壁垒的替换通道是政治性的，时滞可达百年——框架在制度层有方向预测力、无时间预测力。

3.4 Unifying Layer: The Work–Crystallization Theory　3.4 统一层：做功—结晶理论

The two great classes of barriers (individual capability and structural power) are unified as phases of work: gaseous (present only while the work is being done, constrained by 24 hours), liquid (the gray zone: organizational culture, deep relationships), and solid (storable, transferable, inheritable). Crystallization is performed by registration systems; the critical parameter of crystallization is container ownership — the crystallization of strong power sits in self-owned registration systems, while the crystallization of weak power (e.g., platform influencers) sits in rented containers and can be confiscated in an instant. The solid phase exempts one from being present, but not from working: work shifts from generative to maintenance-type, and depreciation comes from three directions — forgetting, institutional change, and network migration.

两大类壁垒（个人能力与结构性权力）统一于做功的相态：气态（随做随在，受24小时约束）、液态（灰色带：组织文化、深度关系）、固态（可储存、可转移、可继承）。结晶由登记系统完成；结晶的关键参数是容器所有权——强权力的结晶位于自有登记系统，弱权力（如平台网红）的结晶位于租来的容器，可被瞬间没收。固态免除在场要求，但不免除做功要求：做功由产生型转为维护型，折旧来自遗忘、制度变迁与网络迁移三个方向。

The master formula(†):

总公式(†)：

Returns = f( Historical work input × Crystallization efficiency × Container ownership ,  Maintenance work ÷ Depreciation rate of the stock )

收益 = f（ 历史做功总量 × 结晶效率 × 容器所有权 ，  维护做功 ÷ 存量折旧率 ）

An equivalent reading: returns ≈ degree of dependence of the system on you × the speed at which that dependence can be dissolved × pricing power − maintenance costs. The four variables cover all attribute differences between the two classes: the container of Class 1 (individual capability) is the body — small capacity, fast depreciation, non-transferable, returns linear in 24 hours; the container of Class 2 (structural power) is the structure itself, whose maintenance cost can be externalized to the system — the only form of returns that can exceed individual service limits without bound.

等价读法：收益 ≈ 被系统依赖的程度 × 依赖被解除的速度 × 定价权 − 维护成本。四变量覆盖两大类的全部属性差异：第一类（个人能力）的容器为身体，容量小、折旧快、不可转让，收益线性于24小时；第二类（结构性权力）的容器为结构，维护成本可外部化给系统，是唯一可无限超出个人服务限度的收益形态。

3.5 Boundary Layer: Ripples, the Lower Bound, and Automation　3.5 边界层：涟漪、下界与自动化

Ripples = Gradient × Search. The gradient (fuel) comes entirely from the Earth–Sun physical layer; the search (ignition) is implemented by materialized information systems — brains, scientific communities, R&D organizations — with consciousness as the searcher rather than the prime mover. In a homogeneous system, ripples are endogenous: the homogenizing offensive pushes the system away from equilibrium, and tiny fluctuations are spontaneously amplified into new barriers (the mechanism of spontaneous symmetry breaking) — flattening and creating are two phases of the same process(†).

涟漪 = 梯度 × 搜索。梯度（燃料）全部来自地球—太阳物理层；搜索（点火）由大脑、科学共同体与研发组织等物质化的信息系统实现，意识是搜索器而非原动力。均匀系统中涟漪是内生的：均一化攻势把系统推离平衡，微小涨落被自发放大为新壁垒（对称性自发破缺机制）——抹平与创造是同一过程的两个相位(†)。

The lower bound of differentiation is above zero: homogenization is asymptotic, never terminal. The strict form of the lower bound is the non-homogenizability of decision rights (one decision cannot be made by everyone simultaneously) [Arrow 1951; Radner 1993], plus physical constraints (embodiment, energy gradients). The lower bound is not a ruin; it is the last position each generation stands on.

差异下界大于零：均一化渐近而非到达。下界的严格形式是决策权不可均分（一个决定不能由所有人同时做出），加上物理约束（具身性、能量梯度）。下界不是废墟，是每代人最后站的位置。

Automation is the extracorporeal crystallization of work (an endogenous tendency): memory → writing → databases; computation → computers; generation → models. With each externalization, the price of that capability falls to zero, and the human position retreats one level along the embeddedness axis. The executor of each automation wave is systematically the target of the next (blacksmith → mechanic → programmer) — zero counterexamples in two centuries(†). The AI era's specificity is only a change of parameters: depreciation is an order of magnitude faster, and externalization touches "generation" for the first time; but generation was never the lower bound of barriers — only the latest position of the formalizable frontier.

自动化是做功的体外结晶（内生倾向）：记忆→文字→数据库，计算→计算机，生成→模型。每次体外化，该能力价格归零，人的位置沿嵌入度轴退守一层。每个自动化浪潮的执行者，系统性地是下一个浪潮的被自动化者（铁匠→机械师→程序员）——两百年间零反例(†)。AI 时代的特殊性仅为参数变化：折旧速度上了一个数量级，体外化首次触及"生成"；但生成从不是壁垒下界，只是可形式化边界的最新推进位置。

4 A Theory of Choice　4 选择理论

4.1 The Degrees of Freedom of Choice　4.1 选择的自由度

Choice is effective on only two degrees of freedom: position (the three-dimensional coordinates of a barrier) and search (the betting rate on new ripples). Choice determines the structure and the exposure of one's returns, not the overall water level. Embeddedness and binding are two readings of the same variable: any position with persistently high returns is in fact paid for with freedom — the high returns at a barrier are, in essence, society's compensation for "being willing to be bound there, working continuously."

选择只在两个自由度上有效：位置（壁垒的三维坐标）与搜索（对新涟漪的下注率）。选择决定收益的结构与暴露方式，不决定总量的水位。嵌入度与绑定是同一变量的两个读数：任何持久高收益的位置都在事实上以自由支付——壁垒处的高收益，本质是社会为"愿意被绑在那里持续做功"支付的补偿。

4.2 Six Core Strategies　4.2 六条核心策略

  1. Position on a layer with "high pressure × slow attack speed." Migrate toward layers that are "hard to learn, hard to imitate, hard to take away": trust, judgment, embodiment, tacit knowledge, responsibility-bearing signatures.
  2. Flowing with the current beats blocking it. Make things that make other people's flows cheaper, and collect tolls on the channel; do not build roadblocks. Historically, those who blocked flows died very uniformly (guilds vs. money; the Luddites vs. the factory owners).
  3. The objectification test. If the core of a barrier can be fully described by a database, a scoring system, or a prompt, its rent is already decaying.
  4. Convert one-time income into a toll gate. Restructure one-off income into a checkpoint that skims every flow.
  5. Search strategy: "rather be wrong early than right late." The first fluctuation to be amplified takes the positive feedback and path dependence; the expectation depends on who gets amplified first, not on who ends up best. Bet small, bet often, bet broadly.
  6. Manage a portfolio across embeddedness. Homogenizable accounts (for liquidity) and non-homogenizable accounts (holding durable barriers) are allocated according to the life cycle.
  1. 卡位"压强大 × 攻速慢"的层：往"学不会、仿不像、抢不走"的层迁移（信任、判断、具身、默会知识、责任签字）。
  2. 顺流优先于阻流：做让别人的流动更便宜的东西，在通道上收费；不做堵路设卡的东西。
  3. 对象化检验：若壁垒核心可被数据库、评分或提示词完整描述，则其租金已在衰减。
  4. 时间结构关卡化：把一次性的收入改建为每次流动抽成的关卡。
  5. 搜索策略"宁可早错，不可晚对"：先发涨落拿走正反馈与路径依赖，期望取决于谁先被放大；下注形态为小成本、高频次、广覆盖。
  6. 嵌入度组合管理：可均一账户（换流动性）与不可均一账户（持有持久壁垒）按生命周期配置。

4.3 Operationalizing the Four Variables　4.3 四变量操作化

Three questions about an occupation: in whose name is your work crystallized; what is the ratio of flow to stock in your pay (what can you take with you if you leave in three years); which depreciation bracket does your skill sit in and how generatable is it. Reviewing employers and platforms: crystallization clauses (authorship, client ownership, non-compete boundaries); container ownership (multi-platform distribution = opening multiple passbooks). Four principles of asset allocation: continuously convert flow income into solid stocks; diversify containers; match depreciation rates to age; always retain gaseous capability. The minimalist version of daily strategy in the AI era: leave generation to the machine, keep judgment to yourself, sign your own name on responsibility.

职业三问：做功结晶在谁名下；工资中流量与存量的比例（三年后离开能带走什么）；技能折旧档位与可生成性。雇主与平台审查：结晶条款（署名权、客户归属、竞业边界）；容器所有权（多平台分发＝多开存折）。资产配置四原则：流量收入持续兑换固态存量；容器分散；折旧率匹配年龄；永远保留气态能力。AI 时代日常策略的极简化版本：生成交给机器，判断留给自己，责任签自己名。

4.4 Validity Across Eras　4.4 跨时代有效性

The six strategies are structurally identical across the four automation waves of agriculture, physical labor, computation, and information — and so are the wrong strategies (deepening into an obsolescent configuration layer, keeping one's passbook in the employer's container, blocking trends with a barrier). Arkwright in 1769 (production to the machine, judgment and responsibility to himself, building his own container) was the earliest complete implementation of this strategy structure; the Luddite movement and the guild path produced zero successful cases in two centuries.

六条策略在农业、体力、计算、信息四轮自动化浪潮中结构一致；错误策略亦一致（在配置层加深到被淘汰、存折开在雇主容器、以壁垒阻挡趋势）。1769 年阿克赖特（生产交给机器、判断与责任留给自己、自建容器）是该策略结构最早的完整实现；卢德运动与行会路径两百年间零成功案例。

Digesting counterexamples is the framework's main mechanism of upgrading. The nurse/teacher cases exposed a missing "pricing power" factor in the formula (ownership of the valve); the "capital idles and wins" case exposed the hidden assumption that "maintenance work can be externalized"; both were digested inside the formula, which after correction reads: long-run returns ≈ societal dependence × time × valve − the bill.

6 Scope Limits and Honest Flags　6 适用边界与诚实标注

The framework's known limits: (1) break points are unpredictable — which fluctuation gets spontaneously amplified is near a lottery; the formula describes the persistence of rent after the break, not the break itself; (2) temporal prediction fails at the institutional layer — the lag is decided by politics; (3) the depreciation of a rented container contains the container owner's will as a variable and cannot be modeled separately.

框架的已知边界：（1）破缺点不可预测——自发破缺选哪个涨落近乎抽签，公式描述破缺后的租金存续；（2）制度层时间预测失效——时滞由政治决定；（3）租赁容器的折旧包含容器主人意志变量，不可单独建模。

The following propositions currently lack direct empirical support and are internal inferences or synthetic innovations of the framework(†): the net-barrier-cost criterion for competition among registration systems; the unified theory of registration systems as crystallizers of work, and the four-variable master formula; the quantitative relation between maintenance work and the depreciation rate; the recursion "executor of an automation wave → target of the next"; the prediction that "the barriers remaining in the AI era = verification + responsibility + embodiment + trust"; and the strong formulation that "flattening and creating are the same phase" of ripples. These propositions constitute the framework's falsifiable frontier.

以下命题暂无直接实证，属框架的内部推论或合成创新(†)：登记系统竞争的净壁垒成本判据；登记系统＝做功结晶器的统一理论与四变量总公式；维护做功÷折旧率的量化关系；自动化浪潮"执行者→被自动化者"的递归规律；"AI 时代剩余壁垒＝验证＋责任＋具身＋信任"的预测；涟漪"抹平与创造同一相位"的强表述。上述命题构成框架的可证伪前沿。

7 Testable Assertions　7 可检验断言

To test the framework's predictive power, we convert its core extrapolative propositions into six falsifiable assertions. The assertions follow the public conventions of forecasting platforms (the criteria–deadline–edge-cases triad of the Metaculus question-writing guide; the "confidence-tagged assertion + next-year retrospective" format of the Astral Codex Ten annual prediction contest, with a sample retrospective). Each assertion is bound to a third-party data source with a clear release cycle; deadlines are back-derived from data availability. We recommend calibrating with Brier scores year by year — if the calibration curve deviates systematically, the framework should be revised, not the assertions. To make the derivation explicit, each assertion below carries a \emph{Framework link} (which propositions entail it) and \emph{Stakes} (what its resolution implies for the framework's validity).

为检验框架的预测能力，我们将框架的核心外推命题转化为六条可证伪断言。断言写作遵循预测平台的公开规范（Metaculus 问题写作指南的判据—截止日—边界情形三要素；Astral Codex Ten 年度预测大赛的"带置信度断言＋次年复盘"模式，复盘示例）。每条断言绑定一个发布周期明确的第三方数据源，截止日按数据可得性倒推；建议以 Brier 分数逐年校准——若校准曲线系统性偏离，应回头修正框架而非修正断言。为说明各断言与框架的推导关系及其证伪后果，以下每条断言均附【框架关联】（框架为何推出此断言）与【输赢影响】（输赢各自如何影响框架的有效性）两项说明。

框架关联：本断言检验容器所有权命题的政治推论——弱权力的结晶寄存于租来的容器、可被瞬间没收，而系统性没收会催生制度层重订登记规则；这是对"登记系统竞争"政治终局的下注。

Stakes: A win supports "registration-system competition is ultimately re-regulated by politics"; a loss mainly weakens the policy relevance of the container-ownership proposition --- yet the framework already concedes the institutional layer has "directional but not temporal predictive power," so damage is low.

输赢影响：赢，则"登记系统竞争最终由政治重订规则"获支持；输，主要弱化容器所有权命题的政策相关性——但框架本就承认制度层"有方向预测力、无时间预测力"，受损程度低。

8 Conclusion　8 结论

Market trade is the flow of potential differentials; a barrier is the crystallization against the current; rent is the compensation for maintaining the crystallization; work is the currency of all barriers; the registration system is the exchange; depreciation is entropy; automation is the ever-lengthening river of externalization. The four variables of individual fate: total historical work input, crystallization efficiency, container ownership, and the ratio of maintenance work to the depreciation rate. To the original question — will returns that exceed personal limits eventually be eliminated? — the corrected answer is: every return that does not attach to continuing personal input will be eliminated, and every return that does attach to it already is, by itself, personal service.

市场交易是势差的流动，壁垒是逆流的结晶，租金是维持结晶的补偿，做功是一切壁垒的货币，登记系统是交换所，折旧是熵，自动化是体外化的长河。个人命运的四个变量：历史做功总量、结晶效率、容器所有权、维护做功与折旧率之比。对"超出个人限度的收益是否终将被消灭"这一最初问题，修正后的回答是：凡不依附于持续个人投入的收益都会被消灭，凡依附的，本身就已是个人服务。
