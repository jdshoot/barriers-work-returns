## 摘要 / Abstract

本文从一个根本问题出发——人类交易市场是如何产生的——构建了一个统一的社会经济分析框架：交易是差异的流动，壁垒是逆均一化趋势维持势差的存在结构，租金是维持壁垒的成本补偿；个人能力与结构性权力统一为"做功—结晶"的相态，收益由历史做功总量、结晶效率、容器所有权、维护做功与折旧率之比四个变量决定。框架附带六条选择策略、十六组文献证据检验，以及六条可证伪的可检验断言（截止 2027–2028 年，绑定 BLS / Census / Stanford 等第三方数据源）。

Starting from the question of how human markets come into existence, the paper builds a unified framework in which trade is the flow of differences, barriers are structures maintaining potential differentials against homogenization, and rent is the compensation for maintaining them. Individual capability and structural power are unified as phases of "work–crystallization," with returns governed by four variables. The framework ships with six strategy rules, sixteen sets of documentary evidence, and six falsifiable testable assertions (deadlines 2027–2028, bound to third-party data sources such as BLS, Census, and Stanford Digital Economy Lab).
